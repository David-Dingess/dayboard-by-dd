The pet in this directory is fan art of Clawd, the Claude Code mascot. Clawd
and Claude are Anthropic's; this is unofficial and not affiliated with or
endorsed by Anthropic.

The pet sprites in this directory (clawd-walk.png, clawd-wave.png,
clawd-sleep.png) are derived from the default pet spritesheet of OpenPets
(https://github.com/OpenPetsHQ/openpets), taken at tag v2.5.1 —
apps/desktop/assets/default-pet-spritesheet.webp.

Changes: three animation rows (walk, wave, sleep) were extracted from the
8x9 grid of 192x208 frames and packed into one horizontal strip each, scaled
to 64x64. Every frame shares one horizontal window, so the pet stays
registered side to side, but each is BOTTOM-ALIGNED individually: the source
does not draw him on a constant baseline (the sleep row's lowest pixel rises
36px as he squashes), and on a shelf his feet have to stay on the line.
Nothing was recoloured.

Note: OpenPets replaced this pet with a different one (an owl) in a later
release. The MIT terms below are the ones that shipped with v2.5.1, which is
the version this artwork comes from.
